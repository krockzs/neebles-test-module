N.E.E.B.L.E.S. Test Module 1.2.5

Changes in this version:
- Persistent Show Tray switch in module-owned Settings.
- Start Tray with Linux switch: hiding the Tray turns it off and disables it.
- Undecorated popup without a close X; dismiss with Escape or focus loss.
- The governed Open surface in the NEEBLES Tray is labeled "Open Test Module" and follows the module's real opened/closed state.
- Existing demo controls and the Tray's independence from the main application are preserved.

After updating, enable "Show Tray" from the module window to show its icon; enable "Start Tray with Linux" if you want it on KDE Plasma login.
